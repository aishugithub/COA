The subject

Why this subject

Computer Organization & Architecture is where the abstraction finally drops away. Students spend years writing code that "just runs"; here they get to see what's really happening beneath it — how instructions move through a datapath, how memory is organised, how the machine is built to be fast. When that click happens for a student, it's one of the most rewarding moments in teaching.

I care about this subject because it rewards curiosity about the invisible. Nothing here is magic once you look closely, and getting students to look closely is exactly the kind of teaching I love.
